## 1. Introduction

Hub by CXLoop ("App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("Hub by CXLoop"), available on the Google Play Store.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Information:**
- Name and email address
- Phone number
- Profile picture (optional)
- Account preferences

**Location Information:**
- Delivery addresses
- Pickup locations
- Location history (if enabled)

**Payment Information:**
- Payment methods are processed by third-party service providers (DoorDash, Uber, etc.)
- We do NOT store full credit card details
- Payment data is encrypted and handled by third parties

**Communication:**
- Messages you send to customer support
- Feedback and reviews you provide
- Preferences for push notifications

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device model and type
- Operating system version
- Device identifier
- IP address
- Mobile network information

**Usage Data:**
- Features you use
- Frequency of use
- Time spent in app
- Screens/pages visited
- Interactions with services

**Location Data (if enabled):**
- GPS coordinates
- Approximate location based on IP address
- Used to show nearby services

**Crash and Performance Data:**
- App crashes and errors
- Performance metrics
- Diagnostic information

## 4. Permissions Required

The Hub by CXLoop app requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose | Required |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **INTERNET** | Connect to services and servers | ✅ Yes |
| **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE** | Check internet connectivity | ✅ Yes |
| **ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION** | Find nearby services | ⚠️ Optional |
| **ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION** | Approximate location | ⚠️ Optional |
| **CAMERA** | Upload photos (if available) | ⚠️ Optional |
| **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE** | Access device files | ⚠️ Optional |
| **WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE** | Save app data | ⚠️ Optional |

**Optional permissions** can be enabled/disabled in app settings.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:
- Provide services
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce agreements

**Retention Periods:**
- Account data: While account is active + 90 days after deletion
- Transaction data: 7 years (for legal/tax compliance)
- Usage data: 12 months
- Crash reports: 30 days

You can request data deletion by contacting us (see Section 11).
